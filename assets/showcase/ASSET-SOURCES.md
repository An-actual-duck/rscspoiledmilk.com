# Showcase Asset Sources

These are web copies of project-owned showcase exports. The source files stay
in the read-only game repository; this directory contains only the files used
by the website prototype and reduced-motion still frames derived with FFmpeg.

## Released assets

- `slayer/monsters/`: all eight GIFs from
  `output/art/showcase/slayer-monsters/`, source commit
  `b283d3e0f7f3c396c9a039bf3f95212ed887adc1`. Their timing is illustrative,
  not a recording of live combat cadence.
- `slayer/equipment/slayer-weapons.gif`: from
  `output/art/showcase/slayer-weapons/v002/`, source commit
  `adfad22d010cab306fd036eda498e855bf623648`. It shows the Abyssal Whip,
  Thunder Spire Staff, and Leaching Bow.
- `slayer/equipment/abyssal-whip-action.gif`: from
  `output/art/showcase/weapons/abyssal-whip/`. The approved art was released
  in v0.3.3 at commit `55ed30a6fa3f2bc732214c7ec93f3c1e0c252e7c`.
- `magic/`: Sacred Unicorn, Thunder Strike, and Zamorak’s Wrath from
  `output/art/showcase/spells-and-summons/v001/`, source commit
  `b31466614322e6e0b6b29f6c6dca45c11fda0da0`. These are offline
  compositions using the real client renderer. Effect timing is authentic;
  pauses between scenes are editorial.
- `items/`: twelve item-variety GIFs from the owner-supplied v002 folder.
  Elemental swords, leather armour sets, sigils, bangles, god maces, and
  shuriken are byte-for-byte identical to their released v001 counterparts.

## Visual-corrections previews

At the owner's direction, the gallery also includes the changed v002 robes,
bolts, crossbows, two staff collections, and ingots. Their source manifest
describes them as a visual-corrections candidate and says the public v0.3.3
deployment is unchanged. The prototype labels the collection as previewing
the next round of sprite touch-ups. Remove that note once the corrected art is
part of a public release.
