# Showcase Asset Sources

These are web copies of project-owned showcase exports. The source files stay
in the read-only game repository; this directory contains only the files used
by the website prototype and reduced-motion still frames derived with FFmpeg.

## Released assets

- `slayer/monsters/`: all eight GIFs from
  `output/art/showcase/slayer-monsters/`, source commit
  `b283d3e0f7f3c396c9a039bf3f95212ed887adc1`. Their timing is illustrative,
  not a recording of live combat cadence.
- `slayer/equipment/slayer-weapons.gif`: from
  `output/art/showcase/slayer-weapons/v002/`, source commit
  `adfad22d010cab306fd036eda498e855bf623648`. It shows the Abyssal Whip,
  Thunder Spire Staff, and Leaching Bow.
- `slayer/equipment/abyssal-whip-action.gif`: from
  `output/art/showcase/weapons/abyssal-whip/`. The approved art was released
  in v0.3.3 at commit `55ed30a6fa3f2bc732214c7ec93f3c1e0c252e7c`.
- `magic/`: Sacred Unicorn, Thunder Strike, and Zamorak’s Wrath from
  `output/art/showcase/spells-and-summons/v001/`, source commit
  `b31466614322e6e0b6b29f6c6dca45c11fda0da0`. These are offline
  compositions using the real client renderer. Effect timing is authentic;
  pauses between scenes are editorial.
- `skills/skills-showcase-loop.mp4`: from
  `output/art/showcase/skills-footage/montage/v006/`. It is a 16.9-second,
  silent H.264 loop showing Blessing, Enchanting, and Leatherworking, with
  SHA-256 `cd6b171c0121201ef1b150ff2bc65030bce8bf7861ce4c8492f5935eab518454`.
  The poster is a web derivative from the Leatherworking portion of the loop.
- `combat/combat-showcase-loop.mp4`: from
  `output/art/showcase/combat-footage/montage/v006/`. It is a 19.9-second,
  silent H.264 loop showing multi-target Melee, Ranged, and Magic with a
  scythe, shuriken, and Iban Blast. SHA-256:
  `9c07e50ce0fa3c273332a32202d60fbb8ac87349e0d0b69544f9d0175e4db9cf`.
  The poster is a web derivative from the scythe portion of the loop.
- `world/locations-showcase-crossfade.mp4`: from
  `output/art/showcase/locations-footage/montage/v001/`. It is a 16.5-second,
  silent H.264 crossfade montage of familiar, updated, and new locations.
  SHA-256:
  `f2f5c42a44d08bd93e071aebeec305fb6667476700ea22a978837f7d655bdd5d`.
  The poster is a web derivative from the opening location.
- `items/`: twelve item-variety GIFs from the owner-supplied v002 folder, plus
  the v0.3.4 Herblaw ingredients loop. Elemental swords, leather armour sets,
  sigils, bangles, god maces, and shuriken are byte-for-byte identical to
  their earlier v001 counterparts.
- `items/herblaw-ingredients.gif`: from
  `output/art/showcase/herblaw-ingredients/v001/`, release v0.3.4, source
  commit `3335addcea396a0dd5069580508233a7617791ba`.

## Visual-corrections history

The changed v002 robes, bolts, crossbows, two staff collections, and ingots
were initially imported as previews. Their corrected visuals were published
in v0.3.4 and no longer require a development label.
